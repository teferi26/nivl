2. Purpose/audience
Spanish-language habit and personal organisation app for people aged 16+. Routines, projects, schedules and progress; optional exercise, nutrition, study, journal and personal finance records. Tracking is free; AI coaching is optional and paid. Available to consumers, not restricted to businesses/employees.

3. Access/main features
Use the credentials in App Review's sign-in fields. NIVL has its own account (email and password, direct NIVL login). No external hardware, files, bank connection or personal AI API key is required.
Confirm age if prompted. Hoy shows missions; Hábitos manages routines; Agenda organises scheduled activities. Perfil provides permissions, export and account deletion. Health and AI require separate explicit permissions; declining leaves general organisation available. Permissions can be withdrawn from Perfil.
New in 1.0.11, modes: Perfil > Tu arsenal (also on Hoy), or tap the centre emblem of the tab bar to open the mode wheel, switches between NIVL (general), IRON (training and diet), FOCUS (study), WORK (work and finances) and FOUNDER (projects and finances). Each mode has its own Hoy, tabs and coach; the emblem returns to NIVL.
