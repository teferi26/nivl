PERMISSIONS AND COACH
Health: Perfil > "Salud y bienestar" > "Revisar permiso de salud" > tick > "Aceptar y activar salud". AI: separate consent, "Acepto y activo el coach". To try the coach, accept both. Voice (Pro/Élite): hold the microphone button in Coach to dictate; "Escuchar" reads a reply. The microphone is used only while the button is held.

PURCHASES
Last onboarding step, or Perfil > "NIVL Pro" > "Pro" or "Élite" and a plan. Products: nivl_pro_mensual, nivl_pro_anual, nivl_elite_mensual, nivl_elite_anual, nivl_elite_fundador (1 year). The screen shows title, duration, price, renewal terms, "Restaurar compras", Terms, Privacy and Apple EULA. "Probar el coach 7 días": one-time Pro-level trial, no payment details, no renewal.

PROGRESS PHOTOS (18+)
"Avances" > "Fotos de progreso". Requires an 18+ confirmation and the health permission. Private storage, screenshots blocked on that screen, never analysed by AI.

FRIENDS, LEAGUES AND DUELS
Perfil > "Amigos y duelos": ranking, private leagues and weekly duels based on consistency (nothing staked). Report a user with "Enviar denuncia" or block with "Bloquear usuario"; "Convivencia y seguridad" has "Desbloquear" and support. AI replies: "Denunciar respuesta". The developer checks the queue daily and acts within 24 hours. No user-to-user messaging.

SHARING
Level, rank, week, before/after and recap cards (9:16 or 4:5) through the system share sheet. Alias, photos and weight are off by default.

IPAD
Supported, portrait, with side navigation.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Perfil > "Cuenta" > "Eliminar cuenta" > "Eliminar para siempre" (sheet) > "Eliminar para siempre" (alert). Deletes the NIVL account, its data and files. Please test with a new account.

CREATORS
Invite-only creator accounts see a read-only progress panel. Earnings are handled outside the app.

SERVICES
Supabase (Frankfurt), Resend (account emails), Apple IAP + RevenueCat, DeepSeek (Pro, trial) and Anthropic (Élite) after AI consent, Apple speech services, Expo/APNs. No analytics, ads or tracking SDKs.
