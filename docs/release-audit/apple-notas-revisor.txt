WHAT IT IS
NIVL is a Spanish-language habit and organisation app for individual users aged 16+. Habits, projects, calendar, training, nutrition, journal and finances are free. Optional auto-renewable subscriptions (Pro, Élite) add an AI coach.

ACCOUNTS
Free account: credentials in the sign-in fields. Use it for the purchase, sandbox payment and "Restaurar compras".
Pro account: [PRO_USER] / [PRO_PASS]
Élite account: [ELITE_USER] / [ELITE_PASS]
Pro and Élite access was granted manually on our server. Please do not buy or restore with these two accounts: a sandbox purchase would replace the granted access, which would then expire.
Sign in (all accounts): "ENTRAR" tab > email and password > "ENTRAR". New account: "CREAR CUENTA" tab > details > tick Terms/Privacy > "CREAR CUENTA" > open the email confirmation link on the device > sign in.
None of the accounts has accepted the age declaration or the health and AI consents; the app asks for them on first use.

PERMISSIONS AND COACH
Health: Perfil > "Salud y bienestar" > "Revisar permiso de salud" > tick the box > "Aceptar y activar salud". AI: separate consent, "Acepto y activo el coach". Declining keeps the free features. To try the coach, accept both, then open Coach and send, for example, "Ayúdame a organizar mi día de mañana".

PURCHASES
Perfil > "NIVL Pro" row (below the profile header) > choose "Pro" or "Élite" and a plan. During the trial, tap "Suscribirme"; Pro subscribers can tap "Ver NIVL Élite". Products: nivl_pro_mensual (1 month), nivl_pro_anual (1 year), nivl_elite_mensual (1 month), nivl_elite_anual (1 year), nivl_elite_fundador (1 year, limited). The screen shows title, duration and price, renewal terms, "Restaurar compras", and Terms and Privacy links. "Probar el coach 7 días": one-time 7-day Pro-level coach trial with a small usage cap; no payment details, no renewal or conversion, no Élite features. Afterwards the coach requires an In-App Purchase subscription.

REPORT AND BLOCK
Perfil > "Amigos" > another user's three-dot button (or their row in a ranking) > reason > "Enviar denuncia". The same sheet has "Bloquear usuario" > "Bloquear". "Convivencia y seguridad" has "Desbloquear" and "Contactar con soporte". The developer checks the queue personally every day and acts within 24 hours. Names and photos stay private until approved. AI coach replies can be reported with "Denunciar respuesta". There is no user-to-user messaging.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Perfil > "Cuenta" section > "Eliminar cuenta" > "Eliminar para siempre" (sheet) > "Eliminar para siempre" (alert). It deletes the NIVL account, its data and files. Please test deletion with a newly created account, not the review accounts. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription. "Exportar mis datos" is in the same section.

SERVICES
Supabase (Frankfurt): auth, data, storage, functions. Resend: account emails. Apple IAP + RevenueCat: subscriptions. AI after consent: DeepSeek (Pro and trial), Anthropic (Élite and short tasks). No analytics, ads or tracking SDKs. Expo/APNs: optional notifications and updates. Vercel: web pages.

REGIONS
Same features in all 165 enabled territories; Spanish interface; prices follow the storefront.

REGULATED / THIRD-PARTY
No regulated activity: no medical diagnosis, financial advice, banking or gambling; no HealthKit. Fonts under the SIL OFL 1.1.
