WHAT IT IS
NIVL is a Spanish-language habit and organisation app for individual users aged 16+. Habits, projects, calendar, training, nutrition, journal and finances are free. Optional auto-renewable subscriptions (Pro, Élite) add an AI coach.

PERMISSIONS AND COACH
Health: Perfil > "Salud y bienestar" > "Revisar permiso de salud" > tick the box > "Aceptar y activar salud". AI: separate consent, "Acepto y activo el coach". Declining keeps the free features. Then open Coach and send, for example, "Ayúdame a organizar mi día de mañana".

PURCHASES
Perfil > "NIVL Pro" row (below the profile header) > choose "Pro" or "Élite" and a plan. During the trial, tap "Suscribirme"; Pro subscribers can tap "Ver NIVL Élite". Products: nivl_pro_mensual (1 month), nivl_pro_anual (1 year), nivl_elite_mensual (1 month), nivl_elite_anual (1 year), nivl_elite_fundador (1 year, limited). The screen shows title, duration and price, renewal terms, "Restaurar compras", and Terms and Privacy links. "Probar el coach 7 días" is a server trial: no payment details and no renewal.

REPORT AND BLOCK
Perfil > "Amigos" > another user's three-dot button (or their row in a ranking) > reason > "Enviar denuncia". The same sheet has "Bloquear usuario" > "Bloquear". "Convivencia y seguridad" has "Desbloquear" and "Contactar con soporte". A person reviews reports. Names and photos stay private until approved. There is no user-to-user messaging.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Perfil > "Cuenta" section > "Eliminar cuenta" > "Eliminar para siempre" (sheet) > "Eliminar para siempre" (alert). [DELETION_SHORT] Please test deletion with a newly created account, not the review accounts. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription. "Exportar mis datos" is in the same section.

SERVICES
Supabase (Frankfurt): auth, data, storage, functions. Franky account service (ours). Apple IAP + RevenueCat: subscriptions. AI after consent: DeepSeek (Pro and trial), Anthropic (Élite and short tasks). No analytics, ads or tracking SDKs. Expo/APNs: optional notifications and updates. Vercel: web pages.
