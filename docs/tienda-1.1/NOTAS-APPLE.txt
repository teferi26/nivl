WHAT IT IS
Habit and personal organisation app for people 16+ (declared at entry; body photos 18+). Habits, planning, progress and manual logs are free. Optional paid modes add an AI coach. Labels below are quoted in Spanish. [CAMPOS: if languages are on, add "UI follows the device language"]

ACCESS
Credentials are in the App Review sign-in fields. Cover > "Ya tengo cuenta" > "Entrar". Confirm age if asked. No permission is pre-accepted. No hardware or bank needed. [CAMPOS: a free account and an all-modes account, both in the private fields, with a demo friend]
Perfil = profile icon, top right of Hoy.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Perfil > Cuenta > "Tu suscripción". Pick one mode or all four, "Anual" or "Mensual". Prices come from StoreKit; annual has a 7-day intro offer if eligible. Another single mode replaces the current one. The AI consent sheet comes before payment. "Restaurar", Terms, Privacy and EULA are on that screen. Older plans are not offered.
Ten product IDs: nivl_{iron,focus,work,founder,todos}_{mensual,anual}.

HEALTHKIT
Why: show the user's activity and avoid double logging. Reads what the user selects: workouts and routes, heart rate, resting heart rate, active energy, steps, sleep, weight. Writes workouts, routes and distance only if enabled. Perfil > "Salud y bienestar" > "Revisar permiso de salud", tick, "Aceptar y activar salud". Then "Conexión con Salud" > "Activar la medición" > "Continuar a Salud" > Apple's dialog. If refused, the app works with manual logs. The UI says "Salud de Apple". No ads, no iCloud.

BACKGROUND GPS
Why: distance, pace and route with the phone pocketed. Hoy > "Cardio" > "Medir con el GPS del iPhone" (needs the steps above). "Continuar", allow While Using, "Carrera" or "Bici", "Empezar". Blue indicator on. "Terminar" stops it. Runs only in a session the user starts; Always is never requested.

WATCH
Optional companion: missions, streak, FOCUS and rest timers, workouts with HealthKit and watch GPS, complication. Modes: workout-processing; mindfulness for the timers. Without a Watch the iPhone app is complete.

AI
Anthropic (Claude) only. A sheet names it, the data including health data, and the countries: "Acepto y activo el coach". Sent: name, missions, agenda with connected calendar titles and times, journal, weight, messages, attached photos, measured workouts with average and max heart rate. Daily Health summary only with the separate switch "Compartir con el coach", off by default. Never GPS routes, heart-rate series or email. Withdraw: Perfil > "Datos y la IA". Each reply has "Denunciar respuesta". After 3 full days without reply the coach pauses; "Reactivar coach".

SOCIAL
No person-to-person chat. Alias, title and photo are seen only through requests, friends and groups, appear at once and are reviewed weekly afterwards. Arena > "Ajustes de la arena" > a user's row or three dots > "Enviar denuncia" or "Bloquear usuario". "Convivencia y seguridad" has "Contactar con soporte".

DELETION
Perfil > Cuenta > "Eliminar cuenta", type ELIMINAR, "Eliminar para siempre", confirm. Use a disposable account. It does not cancel a store subscription.

NOTICES
Not a medical, nutritional or psychological service: stated at onboarding, in Cardio and in Perfil. Finance only records entries or a file the user imports: no bank connection, never moves money, no investment advice.

NOTHING HIDDEN
Server switches turn modes and languages on for everyone; review state is release state. Staff-only panels (server-enforced): moderation, metrics. Creator panel: enrolled creators only.

PLATFORMS
iPhone and iPad, optional Watch companion; no other platform app. [CAMPOS: Mac and Apple Vision: "Not offered in this version" if unticked]
